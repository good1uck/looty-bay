## Renting a plot

The [Districts, Settlements & Base](https://kefirgames.helpshift.com/hc/en/14-rob-run/section/136-districts-settlements-base/) section says you can only set up a base in a settlement that has an owner. Find a vacant plot and pay rent at the sign next to it. The Help Center notes that plots in different levels exist; the excerpt we have does not publish a full price table, so we do not invent rents.

The [November 4, 2026 announcement](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/4262460/view/1848557880479055) says Tier 1 settlements and land rentals now cost **documents** instead of cash. Unlock and purchase costs of certain items in Tier 2 and Tier 3 settlements were increased. That post does not explain how to obtain those documents.

Kefir’s [Getting Started](https://kefirgames.helpshift.com/hc/en/14-rob-run/section/131-getting-started/) section lists “run production at your base” as one path after the intro quests. The November 4 notes mention base businesses including **farms**, **labs**, and **contraband**, and say their profits were massively increased. A [November 20 patch](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/4262460/view/1849827110432538) added a warning when a player who does not have a home location is about to buy contraband.

## Owning a settlement

The same Help Center section says: buy a settlement with rentable plots, and you will receive a cut of the money your tenants spend and launder. You have to collect your cut in person. The November 20 patch also hides settlement owners’ nicknames after they exit the server, to avoid confusion when they re-enter the same server.

## Defense and raids

To protect a base, the Help Center says to install **fuses** in your **Security System**: attackers will have to spend far more time and resources trying to hack it. The same article also mentions defense modules. Official pages do not publish fuse costs in the text we checked, so we omit them.

To raid another player’s base, use a **decoder** on their Security System to gain temporary access. Act fast: the owners can repair the system. The November 4 announcement increased the maximum stack size for decoders, gold bars, and several other items, but did not publish the new stack caps.

You can store stolen goods at your base, but other players can raid you. Cash itself can only be stored at Lenny’s. See the [money guide](/en/guides/money/).

In the November 13 FAQ, Kefir said property fences were not designed to be the ultimate protection — the game already has air vehicles — and that getting over a fence does not automatically grant access to what is inside buildings. They said they plan to make it tougher to breach someone else’s territory on foot or by car.

## Unlocking goods (Shipments)

The Help Center says to check the **Shipments** tab by pressing **H**, or by opening the side menu, in the desert, Lenny’s Motel, or Looty Bay. Unlocking specific items requires further steps; the excerpt does not list every unlock tree, so this site does not invent one. **H** is an official keybind from that Help Center article. We do not add other keys unless an official page names them.

## Vehicles and a helicopter (short version)

The [Transport](https://kefirgames.helpshift.com/hc/en/14-rob-run/section/135-transport/) section says:

- If you don’t have an active vehicle, you can rent a car for free at the nearest **Parking Terminal**.
- To get and upgrade your own car, buy a plot of land with a house or garage, purchase your own vehicle, and store its key in your key locker. Vehicle modules boost performance.
- A **helicopter** is described as a status symbol. You can only buy one if your base is in the **central district**. The Help Center also says that if you are lucky, you can find one in loot.
- You can buy a lockpick on the Black Market to crack other players’ vehicle trunks, including cop cars.

The November 4 announcement says the **Buletti** can no longer transport shipments.
