This page only summarizes what Kefir has posted on the [ROB RUN Steam news page](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/4262460), the [Steam store page](https://store.steampowered.com/app/4262460/ROB_RUN/), and the [Help Center](https://kefirgames.helpshift.com/hc/en/14-rob-run/). Each entry links to the original post, and the official post is the one to trust. Source check: November 26, 2026 (verified as of November 26, 2026). This is an unofficial fan site.

## How long Early Access is expected to last

In the Early Access section of the [Steam store page](https://store.steampowered.com/app/4262460/ROB_RUN/), Kefir writes: "We expect ROB RUN to be in Early Access for approximately 6–12 months, depending on player feedback and how development progresses." The same section says the game is "fully playable" with the core gameplay in place, but "still needs further polishing, balancing and additional features," and that players "may encounter occasional bugs."

For the full version, Kefir lists plans for additional maps, levels, or game modes; more characters, customization options, and items; progression systems, rankings, or achievements; improved matchmaking, performance, and network stability; and overall polish based on community feedback. Kefir also says it does not plan to change the price when the game leaves Early Access. More on this is in [What is ROB RUN?](/en/guides/what-is-rob-run/).

## Where updates are announced

The Help Center article [Will the game receive updates?](https://kefirgames.helpshift.com/hc/en/14-rob-run/faq/1366-will-the-game-receive-updates/) answers: "Yes, the game will receive regular updates." It says all announcements are posted in the official communities: [Discord](https://discord.gg/robrun), [X](https://x.com/ROBRUNGame), the [Steam page](https://store.steampowered.com/app/4262460/ROB_RUN/), [Facebook](https://www.facebook.com/robrungame), [Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/robrungame), [VK](https://vk.ru/robrungame), and [Telegram](https://t.me/robrungame).

The Steam store page also says Kefir will "share regular updates and patch notes." We have not found an official fixed patch schedule (for example, a set weekday), so we do not guess one. The log below lists the dates on which change posts actually appeared.

## Patch log (Steam announcements since launch)

Dates are the Steam posting dates. Each list is a short summary of the full post, so read the linked post for the exact wording.

### November 4, 2026: First Changes & Server Schedule

[Read the post](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/4262460/view/1848557880479055). This was the launch-day list of changes made after the playtest:

- **Cops:** longer cop respawn time, getting longer with each consecutive death; cops can buy police badges, and the selection depends on the district they live in, while badges from looting and cop salaries were significantly reduced; on a fresh server, patrol cops only have access to pistols, and better gear unlocks as the server gets older.
- **Economy and progression:** Tier 1 settlements and land rentals cost documents instead of cash; profits from base businesses (farms, labs, contraband, and more) were massively increased; more cash in dumpsters; dismantling and selling shipments is much more profitable, but shipments drop less often and take longer to dismantle; higher unlock and purchase costs for certain Tier 2 and Tier 3 settlement items; a cheaper basic pistol and more starter weapon spawn points around the city.
- **Items and other changes:** the Buletti can no longer transport shipments; less time needed to crack the Fabergé Egg, but more tablets needed to trade for a glass cutter; the laundering desk can hold much more cash; bigger maximum stacks for decoders, gold bars, and several other items.
- **Server schedule:** two regions, EU and East US, with at least one fresh server per day in each region (EU at 5 PM UTC, East US at 8 PM ET) and additional servers opening automatically as needed. See the [Federal Raid and servers guide](/en/guides/wipe/).

Kefir added that "None of these changes are set in stone."

### November 13, 2026: Answering Your Most Frequently Asked Questions

[Read the post](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/4262460/view/1849192144187218). This was not a patch. It explained what Kefir plans to change and what it is leaving as it is for now. The planned items are listed in the next section.

### November 17, 2026: Patch Notes

[Read the post](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/4262460/view/1849458702693254). The full list of changes:

- a profanity filter for character names;
- a more noticeable Report button on the death screen;
- Shipments added to Lenny's context menu;
- progress and Battle Pass points now carry over if you change regions;
- an option to invert the mouse Y-axis;
- daily and general limits for Jail Time;
- players who don't own a land plot or haven't accumulated any Jail Time yet will not have access to voice chat in the Prison or at Lenny's Motel;
- Eviction Rights temporarily removed from barter;
- improved connection stability.

### November 20, 2026: Patch Notes

[Read the post](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/4262460/view/1849827110432538). The full list of changes:

- more methods of cheater detection;
- online friends are now shown in the server list, so you can see which server they are on;
- the game checks for vacant cop spots before the equipment selection window can be opened;
- a more user-friendly customization process;
- more sets, including some with single skins;
- settlement owners' nicknames are hidden after they leave the server, to avoid confusion when they rejoin the same server;
- a warning when a player without a home location is about to buy contraband;
- localization edits;
- improved network performance.

### November 23, 2026: Anti-Cheat Measures and Servers

[Read the post](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/4262460/view/1849827110441426). This post announced a new wave of bans and said more than 200,000 accounts had been blocked. It was not a patch list. Details and the planned restricted servers are covered in the [anti-cheat and bans guide](/en/guides/anti-cheat-bans/).

Kefir's [November 24, 2026 post](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/4262460/view/1849827110446112) asked players to nominate ROB RUN in the Steam Awards and did not list any game changes.

## Announced, but not shipped yet

These come from official posts and are plans, not features. None has an announced release date unless one is stated.

- **Weapon repair:** not possible at the moment, but "already planned for future updates" ([November 13 FAQ](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/4262460/view/1849192144187218); see [party size and gear durability](/en/guides/party-and-gear-durability/)).
- **Simpler server selection:** Kefir plans to make it "much simpler and more straightforward." For now, switching means surrendering and going back to prison (November 13 FAQ).
- **Police duty:** Kefir says it "will definitely work on improving police duty," because waiting for an open Police Officer slot is inconvenient (November 13 FAQ).
- **Property defenses:** Kefir will work on making it harder to breach someone else's territory on foot or by car (November 13 FAQ).
- **Valuable items:** Kefir will look into making the purpose of valuable items clearer and protecting them from accidental sale (November 13 FAQ).
- **Asia or Oceania servers:** being explored, but "such an expansion won't be possible until at least the end of this year" (November 13 FAQ).
- **Anti-cheat:** hardware bans are being explored, and separate restricted servers that require either a minimum amount of jail time or a certain total of in-game purchases are planned first. Kefir says it is temporarily delaying some upcoming in-game mechanics to prioritize this ([November 23 post](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/4262460/view/1849827110441426)).

## What we do not know

Kefir has not published version numbers, patch sizes, a roadmap with dates, or a date for leaving Early Access beyond the 6–12 month estimate. We update this page when a new official post appears.
