The Help Center does not publish a single mandatory “do these seven steps in order” tutorial beyond the in-game intro quests. What follows is only what official pages actually state, so you can make a first-hour plan without invented routes.

## After the intro quests

In [Getting Started](https://kefirgames.helpshift.com/hc/en/14-rob-run/section/131-getting-started/), Kefir says that after the intro quests you pick a path: pull off heists, haul cargo, run production at your base, join the police, or fight for territory. It is a sandbox — the Help Center does not say you must do those in a fixed order.

## What you lose if you die

This matters immediately. The article [What do I lose when I die?](https://kefirgames.helpshift.com/hc/en/14-rob-run/faq/1344-what-do-i-lose-when-i-die/) says it depends on location and circumstances:

- In **Looty Bay**, Criminals lose their inventory on death, but can surrender to the Police to save their equipped gear.
- In the **desert**, death always means losing all your loot.
- **Civilians** who die in the city keep their bag contents and equipped gear.
- **Police Officers** only lose department-issued gear; their personal items and equipment remain untouched.

In a [November 4, 2026 announcement](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/4262460/view/1848557880479055), Kefir said they considered giving players a pistol after every death and decided not to, because “working your way up from nothing is an important part of ROB RUN.” They also said they made weapons easier to find.

## Weapons, money, and a bank account

The Getting Started section says weapons spawn as city loot and can also be bought on the **Black Market** — either directly from **Lenny** or through the laptop at your base. Official pages do not publish a starter buy list, so we do not invent one.

To earn, the Help Center lists official methods: collect and sell loot, haul and flip cargo, produce goods at your base, or join the police. Settlement owners and clans that control territories also collect taxes. Details are on the [money guide](/en/guides/money/).

To open a bank account, [visit the Looty Bay Bank as a Civilian](https://kefirgames.helpshift.com/hc/en/14-rob-run/faq/1355-how-do-i-open-a-bank-account-and-why-launder-money/), then run cash through the **Laundering Desk** at your base. Laundering takes time. [Cash can be stolen or confiscated](https://kefirgames.helpshift.com/hc/en/14-rob-run/faq/1356-can-my-money-be-stolen/); banked money is safe from theft until the next wipe. You can only [store cash at Lenny’s](https://kefirgames.helpshift.com/hc/en/14-rob-run/section/132-looting-and-robbing/); stolen goods can go in your base, but other players can raid you.

The same November 4 announcement says Tier 1 settlements and land rentals now cost **documents** instead of cash. It does not explain, in that post, how those documents drop, so we do not invent a farming route.

## Servers and meeting friends

Kefir currently lists two regions, EU and East US. Fresh-server times and the Asia/Oceania question are covered in the [wipe and servers guide](/en/guides/wipe/). If a friend is already on another server, the official way to switch is in [troubleshooting](/en/guides/troubleshooting/).
