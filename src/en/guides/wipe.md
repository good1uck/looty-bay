## What the Federal Raid is

The Help Center article [What is the Federal Raid (Wipe)?](https://kefirgames.helpshift.com/hc/en/14-rob-run/faq/1346-what-is-the-federal-raid-wipe/) says:

Every seven days, all plots, bases, weapons, and cash are wiped and converted into **jail time** and **authority**. Your cosmetics, player name, tutorial progress, settings, and accumulated jail time carry over. Each wipe “levels the playing field for a brand-new power struggle.”

The Steam store page describes the same loop in marketing language: in one week you can go from a regular thug to the boss of a large organization, then start over. The Help Center article is the more precise ruleset, so use that when you plan.

Official pages do not, in the sources we checked, publish a universal countdown clock for every server, or a list of the exact items that become jail time versus authority. We do not invent those details. Banked money is [safe from theft until the next wipe](https://kefirgames.helpshift.com/hc/en/14-rob-run/faq/1356-can-my-money-be-stolen/) — it is not safe from the wipe itself.

## Server regions and fresh servers

In [ROB RUN: First Changes & Server Schedule](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/4262460/view/1848557880479055) (November 4, 2026), Kefir wrote:

- The game currently has two regions: **EU** and **East US**.
- “We’ll add more regions when we can.”
- They plan to open **at least one fresh server per day in each region**: EU at **5 PM UTC** (12:00 PM Eastern), East US at **8 PM Eastern**.
- Additional servers will open automatically as needed, including outside those scheduled times.

In [ROB RUN: Answering Your Most Frequently Asked Questions](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/4262460/view/1849192144187218) (November 13, 2026), Kefir answered “Do you plan to open servers in other regions, such as Asia or Oceania?” They said they see the requests and are exploring the technical possibilities, but “such an expansion won't be possible until at least the end of this year.”

The same November 4 announcement says that when a fresh server opens, **patrol cops will only have access to pistols**, and better gear unlocks as the server gets older.

A later [November 20, 2026 patch](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/4262460/view/1849827110432538) added online friends to the servers list, so you can see which server a friend is already in. Switching to that server still follows the Help Center method described in [troubleshooting](/en/guides/troubleshooting/) unless Kefir publishes a simpler selector — in the November 13 FAQ they said they plan to make server selection simpler, because you currently have to surrender and head back to prison.
