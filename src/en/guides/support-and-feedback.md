This page collects what Kefir's [ROB RUN Help Center](https://kefirgames.helpshift.com/hc/en/14-rob-run/) says about contacting support, reporting bugs, and sharing ideas. Everything below is quoted or closely paraphrased from the linked articles. Source check: November 25, 2026 (verified as of November 25, 2026). This is an unofficial fan site; we are not Kefir and cannot answer support requests.

## Support email

[How do I contact support and find official communities?](https://kefirgames.helpshift.com/hc/en/14-rob-run/faq/1381-how-do-i-contact-support-and-find-official-communities/) gives the ROB RUN support address as **robrun.support@kefircyprus.com**. Be careful with look-alike addresses: only use the one the Help Center shows.

## Reporting a bug

[Where do I report bugs?](https://kefirgames.helpshift.com/hc/en/14-rob-run/faq/1378-where-do-i-report-bugs/) says to **contact support** (the same email) and to:

- describe **what you were doing before the error occurred**, and
- **attach a screenshot or video**.

Other Help Center articles ask for specific details in specific cases (see the [troubleshooting guide](/en/guides/troubleshooting/)): for crashes, your PC specs, a description of the crash, and the error message text; for an error code that persists, the text copied from the error window; for an Easy Anti-Cheat error, a screenshot and your UID.

## Finding your UID

[Where can I find my ID?](https://kefirgames.helpshift.com/hc/en/14-rob-run/faq/1372-where-can-i-find-my-id/): press **Esc**. Your UID is displayed on the right, under your character name, and the **"Copy UID"** button copies it to your clipboard.

## Missing purchase

[I made a purchase, but haven't received it](https://kefirgames.helpshift.com/hc/en/14-rob-run/faq/1369-i-made-a-purchase-but-haven-t-received-it-what-should-i-do/): restart the game and check your **in-game mail**. If the purchase is still missing, email support and attach your receipt.

To get the receipt, [Where can I find my Steam receipt?](https://kefirgames.helpshift.com/hc/en/14-rob-run/faq/1370-where-can-i-find-my-steam-receipt/) says: open your purchase history, select the transaction, and click "I would like to view or print the receipt for this purchase." A copy of the receipt is also sent to your email.

## Lost progress

[I lost my progress. What should I do?](https://kefirgames.helpshift.com/hc/en/14-rob-run/faq/1371-i-lost-my-progress-what-should-i-do/): first check whether a **wipe** has occurred (see the [Federal Raid guide](/en/guides/wipe/)) and make sure you are logged into the correct Steam account. If the loss is unexpected, give support your **UID, player name, and the approximate time** the loss happened.

## Personal data

[How do I access, update, or delete my personal data?](https://kefirgames.helpshift.com/hc/en/14-rob-run/faq/1373-how-do-i-access-update-or-delete-my-personal-data/): email **privacy@kefirgames.com**. The Help Center notes that deleting personal data is different from resetting game progress.

## Ideas and feedback

[How do I share an idea or feedback?](https://kefirgames.helpshift.com/hc/en/14-rob-run/faq/1367-how-do-i-share-an-idea-or-feedback/) says to drop a message in the relevant thread of the official communities (listed below). Bugs go to the support email; ideas go to the communities.

## Official communities

The Help Center lists these links in the support, feedback, and [updates](https://kefirgames.helpshift.com/hc/en/14-rob-run/faq/1366-will-the-game-receive-updates/) articles. The updates article says all announcements are posted in these communities.

- Discord: [discord.gg/robrun](https://discord.gg/robrun)
- X: [x.com/ROBRUNGame](https://x.com/ROBRUNGame)
- Steam: [ROB RUN on Steam](https://store.steampowered.com/app/4262460/ROB_RUN/) (news posts are on the [Steam news page](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/4262460))
- Facebook: [facebook.com/robrungame](https://www.facebook.com/robrungame)
- Instagram: [instagram.com/robrungame](https://www.instagram.com/robrungame)
- VK: [vk.ru/robrungame](https://vk.ru/robrungame)
- Telegram: [t.me/robrungame](https://t.me/robrungame)

The Help Center list does not include a Chinese-language community, and we know of no official one. Treat any other group that claims to be official with caution.

## Community rules

[What are the community rules?](https://kefirgames.helpshift.com/hc/en/14-rob-run/faq/1374-what-are-the-community-rules/): cheats, exploiting bugs for an advantage, real-money trading, selling accounts, harassment, spam, and doxxing are strictly prohibited, and violations may result in a ban. For how bans and appeals work, see the [anti-cheat and bans guide](/en/guides/anti-cheat-bans/).

## Not officially stated

We found no official response-time target for support, no official list of supported languages for support emails, and no separate in-game bug-report button described in the Help Center. We do not guess at these.
