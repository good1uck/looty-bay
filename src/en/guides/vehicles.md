This page collects what the Help Center’s [Transport](https://kefirgames.helpshift.com/hc/en/14-rob-run/section/135-transport/) section states, plus later Steam posts that mention vehicles. Official pages do not publish a vehicle roster, prices, a map of Parking Terminals, a module list, or keybinds, so those are left out.

## Renting a car

[How do I get a car?](https://kefirgames.helpshift.com/hc/en/14-rob-run/faq/1357-how-do-i-get-a-car/) says: if you do not have an **active vehicle**, you can rent a car for **free** at the nearest **Parking Terminal**. That is the complete official instruction. The article does not say how long a rental lasts, which models are offered, or whether a rented car can be taken by another player, so we do not invent those details.

## Owning and upgrading your own car

[How do I get and upgrade my own car?](https://kefirgames.helpshift.com/hc/en/14-rob-run/faq/1358-how-do-i-get-and-upgrade-my-own-car/) says: buy a **plot of land with a house or garage** to purchase your own vehicle and store its key in your **key locker**. **Vehicle modules** boost performance: install them **under the hood**, **in the trunk**, or **onto the underbody**, depending on the modification.

Official pages do not list which modules exist, what each one does, or how much they cost. Renting a plot is covered in the [bases guide](/en/guides/bases/).

## Helicopters

[How do I get a helicopter?](https://kefirgames.helpshift.com/hc/en/14-rob-run/faq/1359-how-do-i-get-a-helicopter/) calls a helicopter a **status symbol**. You can only **buy** one if your base is in the **central district**. The same article says that if you are lucky, you can also **find one in loot**. It does not publish a drop rate, a price, or a list of other air vehicles.

## Lockpicking other players’ trunks

[How do I lockpick a trunk?](https://kefirgames.helpshift.com/hc/en/14-rob-run/faq/1360-how-do-i-lockpick-a-trunk/) says: buy a **lockpick** on the **Black Market** to crack into other players’ vehicle trunks, including **cop cars**. Official pages do not describe a minigame, a success rate, or which trunks hold loot, so we leave that out.

## Chases, ramps, and property fences

In a [November 13, 2026 FAQ](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/4262460/view/1849192144187218), answering how solo players can deal with a group of cops, Kefir said you can scout your route ahead of time, **hide your getaway car**, disable alarms, or intimidate NPCs. They also said **criminal vehicles are more maneuverable than police cruisers**, and that **ramps scattered around the city** can help you shake off a chase. They did not publish a ramp map or a vehicle stat table.

In the same FAQ, under “Why Can You Jump Over a Property Fence in a Car?”, Kefir said fences were not designed to be the ultimate protection for your territory — especially since the game already has **air vehicles**. Getting over the fence does not automatically grant access to what’s inside your buildings. A fence is meant to make accessing your property harder, not block it off completely. They agreed that property defenses can be improved and said they would work on making it tougher to breach someone else’s territory on foot or by car.

## The Buletti and shipments

A [November 4, 2026 announcement](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/4262460/view/1848557880479055) says the **Buletti** can no longer transport shipments. The Help Center transport articles do not define the Buletti further. In the same November 13 FAQ, while discussing accidental sales of valuable items, Kefir gave this example: a jammer is needed to steal the Buletti from the Grand Hotel. Official pages we checked do not say whether that is the only way to obtain one, so we stop there. Shipments themselves are covered in the [bases guide](/en/guides/bases/).
