This page puts every official statement about dying in ROB RUN in one place: the Kefir [Help Center](https://kefirgames.helpshift.com/hc/en/14-rob-run/) and Kefir's posts on the [Steam news page](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/4262460). Where the official text is thin, we say so instead of filling the gap. Source check: November 27, 2026 (verified as of November 27, 2026, US Eastern). This is an unofficial fan site.

## The short version: it depends on who and where you are

The Help Center article [What do I lose when I die?](https://kefirgames.helpshift.com/hc/en/14-rob-run/faq/1344-what-do-i-lose-when-i-die/) starts with "It all depends on the location and circumstances," and then lists these cases:

| Situation | What the Help Center says |
| --- | --- |
| **Criminal** dying in **Looty Bay** | Loses their inventory, but can surrender to the Police to save their equipped gear. |
| Anyone dying in the **desert** | "Death always means losing all your loot." |
| **Civilian** dying in the city | Keeps their bag contents and equipped gear. |
| **Police Officer** | Only loses department-issued gear. Personal items and equipment stay untouched. |

The article does not say what happens to the lost items afterward (for example, whether other players can pick them up), so we do not describe that.

## Criminals in Looty Bay: surrender or bribe instead of dying

The Help Center describes two ways to keep more than you would by dying:

- **Surrender:** [Why surrender to the cops?](https://kefirgames.helpshift.com/hc/en/14-rob-run/faq/1352-why-surrender-to-the-cops/) says your bag contents are confiscated, but you keep your equipped gear. Kefir's pre-launch [Gameplay Overview](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/4262460/view/1844115010491502) (September 17, 2026) describes it the same way: the cops only confiscate what is in your bag.
- **Bribe:** [Why pay a bribe?](https://kefirgames.helpshift.com/hc/en/14-rob-run/faq/1353-why-pay-a-bribe/) says that if you have money in the bank, you can offer the cop a bribe. If they accept, you keep everything in your bag, and the cop gets "a solid payout."

The [heists guide](/en/guides/heists/) says a Criminal who dies loses everything in their bag, quoting [How do I pull off heists in Looty Bay?](https://kefirgames.helpshift.com/hc/en/14-rob-run/faq/1347-how-do-i-pull-off-heists-in-looty-bay/). Cops have a reason to ask for surrender too: in the [November 13, 2026 FAQ](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/4262460/view/1849192144187218), Kefir said that cops who turn on the siren and demand that a Criminal surrender get extra bonuses. See the [cops guide](/en/guides/cops/).

## The desert

The Help Center says death in the desert always means losing all your loot. Kefir's pre-launch [Global Map post](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/4262460/view/1844751498222037) (September 24, 2026) calls the desert "a lawless wasteland" where "no one is safe" and any player can try to kill you, and says even your own base will not keep you completely secure. Bases are in the desert, so plan trips there around the rule that you lose everything on death. See the [bases guide](/en/guides/bases/).

## Money: cash versus the bank

Death rules are about items, but cash matters too. [Can my money be stolen?](https://kefirgames.helpshift.com/hc/en/14-rob-run/faq/1356-can-my-money-be-stolen/) says cash can be stolen or confiscated, while banked money is safe from theft until the next wipe. [Where should I stash stolen loot?](https://kefirgames.helpshift.com/hc/en/14-rob-run/faq/1348-where-should-i-stash-stolen-loot/) says you can only store cash at Lenny's. The Help Center does not say exactly how much cash you lose when you die, so we do not state it. See the [money guide](/en/guides/money/).

## Durability loss on every death

In the November 13, 2026 FAQ, Kefir said: "Gear loses durability every time you die." Kefir calls this an item sink that keeps the in-game economy balanced. Weapons cannot be repaired yet, but a repair mechanic is planned. Kefir has not said how much durability is lost per death. Details are on the [party size and gear durability page](/en/guides/party-and-gear-durability/).

## Respawning

- **No free pistol after death:** Kefir's [November 1, 2026 Playtest News](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/4262460/view/1847920302245290) described giving players a pistol on joining a server and on every respawn. In the launch-day [November 4, 2026 post](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/4262460/view/1848557880479055), Kefir said they "initially considered giving players a pistol after every death," but decided that "working your way up from nothing is an important part of ROB RUN." Instead, the basic pistol got cheaper and more starter weapon spawn points were added around the city. [Where can I find weapons?](https://kefirgames.helpshift.com/hc/en/14-rob-run/faq/1343-where-can-i-find-weapons/) says weapons spawn as city loot and can be bought on the Black Market, either from Lenny or through the laptop at your base.
- **Your base as a respawn point:** the pre-launch [Base Management post](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/4262460/view/1846651706680688) (October 16, 2026) says: "If you die, your base will become an additional respawn point." No Help Center article repeats this, so treat it as a pre-launch description.
- **Cops:** the November 4 post increased cop respawn time and adds "an even longer delay with each consecutive death." Kefir has not published the exact times. The pre-launch [Frequently Asked Questions post](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/4262460/view/1845383656385404) (October 2, 2026) says cops "respawn right in Looty Bay."
- **Death screen:** the [November 17, 2026 patch](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/4262460/view/1849458702693254) made the Report button on the death screen more noticeable. To report cheaters, see the [anti-cheat and bans guide](/en/guides/anti-cheat-bans/).

## Not officially stated

We found no official text on: what exactly counts as "inventory," "bag," or "equipped gear" for each slot; what happens to dropped items; whether dying affects Notoriety or Jail Time; respawn timers for non-cops; or how to choose a respawn point. We will add these only if Kefir publishes them.
