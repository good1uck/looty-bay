This page collects what official sources say about cheating and bans: the Steam store page, two Help Center articles, and Kefir’s Steam posts up to November 23, 2026. It is not legal advice and does not replace the official text. Official pages do not publish detection methods, and Kefir says it may keep them private, so this page does not speculate about them.

## Easy Anti-Cheat

- The [Steam store page](https://store.steampowered.com/app/4262460/ROB_RUN/) says ROB RUN uses **kernel-level Easy Anti-Cheat** (EAC).
- [What is Easy Anti-Cheat?](https://kefirgames.helpshift.com/hc/en/14-rob-run/faq/1380-what-is-easy-anti-cheat/) says EAC protects the game from cheats and that using them results in a **permanent ban**. If you get an EAC error, send support a screenshot and your UID.

EAC error codes (E-0501, E-0502, E-0503) and their official fixes are in the [troubleshooting guide](/en/guides/troubleshooting/).

## The ROB RUN ban policy

The Help Center’s [ROB RUN Ban Policy](https://kefirgames.helpshift.com/hc/en/14-rob-run/faq/1384-rob-run-ban-policy/) (in the Account section) says:

- Kefir can restrict access if a player breaks the ROB RUN Terms and Conditions and/or the Steam Online Conduct rules.
- A ban can last **from 24 hours to 5 years**. Kefir sets the length at its sole discretion, depending on how serious the violation is.
- Bans are applied **without prior notice** and take effect immediately. The ban length is shown in a notification on the game’s main screen for as long as the ban lasts.
- Your character and progress are **not reset** during a ban. But if the ban overlaps a server reset (wipe), your inventory (in-game items, currency, and property) is reset under the normal game rules. See the [Federal Raid guide](/en/guides/wipe/).
- In-game purchases do not exempt anyone from the rules, and a ban is not in itself grounds for a refund of purchases already credited.

Note that the two Help Center articles use different wording: the EAC article says cheating means a permanent ban, while the ban policy describes a range of 24 hours to 5 years for violations in general. We report both as written and do not guess how a specific case is handled.

### Listed reasons for a ban

The ban policy says a ban may be given for, among other things:

- Selling or reselling in-game items or virtual currency, or advertising such services.
- Using data-mining or automation tools (crawlers, spiders, scripts, bots, and so on).
- Interfering with, hacking, or decrypting data sent between the servers and the game, or overloading servers.
- Misusing support: false reports, or profane or abusive language toward support.
- Offering in-game services for payment received outside the game.
- Gaining an unfair advantage in ways the game mechanics do not intend, including stream sniping, exploiting bugs or glitches, making or using cheats, bots, scripts, or macros, manipulating mechanics for unauthorized rewards, bypassing anti-cheat, bypassing account restrictions (for example, smurfing), using extra accounts to get around bans, and helping others do any of these.

### Appeals

If you disagree with a ban, the policy says you can appeal to customer support through [How do I contact support and find official communities?](https://kefirgames.helpshift.com/hc/en/14-rob-run/faq/1381-how-do-i-contact-support-and-find-official-communities/) (support email: **robrun.support@kefircyprus.com**). Kefir may review its decision but says it is not obliged to explain the grounds for a ban or the details of the review.

## Recent anti-cheat news (November 2026)

- The [November 17, 2026 patch](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/4262460/view/1849458702693254) made the **Report** button on the death screen more noticeable.
- The [November 20, 2026 patch](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/4262460/view/1849827110432538) “added more methods of cheaters detection.”
- [Anti-Cheat Measures and Servers](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/4262460/view/1849827110441426) (November 23, 2026) says:
  - A new wave of bans has rolled out, and Kefir has blocked **more than 200,000 accounts** so far.
  - To make anti-cheat the top priority, Kefir is **temporarily delaying** development of some upcoming in-game mechanics. The post does not say which ones.
  - Kefir is discussing more security measures with the EAC team, building its own cheat-detection tools, and expanding its in-house anti-cheat team.
  - Because a normal ban is easy to evade in a free-to-play game, Kefir is **exploring hardware bans**.

### Planned servers with stricter access

The same November 23 post says Kefir plans to launch **separate servers with stricter access requirements** first, because those can be set up faster than hardware bans:

- Depending on the server, access will require **either a minimum amount of jail time or a certain total of in-game purchases**.
- Players on these servers will be **matchmade separately** from standard servers, **including in Looty Bay**.
- Kefir expects this to sharply reduce ban evasion and encounters with cheaters, and says it will keep improving anti-cheat on all servers.

This is a plan, not a released feature as of November 24, 2026 (Beijing time). The post gives **no launch date, no jail-time or purchase thresholds, and no regions**, and it does not say hardware bans are live. We will update this page when Kefir publishes those details. Jail time itself is explained in the [Federal Raid guide](/en/guides/wipe/).

## Not officially stated

We could not find official text on: how to report a cheater beyond the death-screen Report button, how long an appeal takes, how many bans were permanent versus temporary, or when the restricted servers or hardware bans will arrive. Unofficial “unban” services or tools are not something we can verify, and the ban policy lists bypassing restrictions as a violation in itself.
