## How to become a cop

The [Police and Arrests](https://kefirgames.helpshift.com/hc/en/14-rob-run/section/133-police-and-arrests/) section says: if there are open slots, enlist at the **Looty Bay Police Station**. You will be issued standard gear and a patrol vehicle. Leveling up your badge is mentioned there; the Help Center excerpt does not publish a full badge-rank table, so we do not invent one.

A [November 20, 2026 patch](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/4262460/view/1849827110432538) added a check for vacant cop spots **before** the equipment selection window can be opened. That matches a common complaint Kefir addressed in their [November 13 FAQ](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/4262460/view/1849192144187218): it can be inconvenient to wait for an open Police Officer slot. They said increasing the number of cops risks disrupting balance, but they “will definitely work on improving police duty.”

On a **fresh server**, the [November 4 announcement](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/4262460/view/1848557880479055) says patrol cops only have access to pistols at first; better gear unlocks as the server gets older. That post also says cops can now buy police badges, with the selection depending on the district they live in, and that badges earned through looting and cop salaries were significantly reduced.

## Pay and risk

The November 13 FAQ asked why cops earn less than criminals. Kefir said it comes down to high-risk, high-reward: cops get their equipment for free and earn a percentage of the confiscated loot’s value when they arrest a Criminal or seize their bag. You can make a hefty sum by taking down a wealthy robber. If you turn on your siren and demand that the Criminal surrender, you get extra bonuses. They also said people play as cops not just for the money, but because it is a lower-risk role.

The Help Center’s death rules say [Police Officers only lose department-issued gear](https://kefirgames.helpshift.com/hc/en/14-rob-run/faq/1344-what-do-i-lose-when-i-die/); personal items and equipment remain untouched. The November 4 announcement increased cop respawn time, with an even longer delay after each consecutive death.

## Surrender and bribes

From the Police and Arrests section:

- You can **surrender**: bag contents are confiscated, but you keep equipped gear.
- If you have money in the bank, you can offer a **bribe**. If the cop accepts, you keep everything in your bag, and the cop gets a payout.

The Help Center does not publish bribe amounts in the articles we checked.

## Related

Criminals remain a real threat, and Kefir has said the Police are meant to stay a real threat to both parties and solo players. Switching servers and joining friends is covered in [troubleshooting](/en/guides/troubleshooting/).
