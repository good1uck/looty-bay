## Official ways to earn

The Help Center article [How do I make money?](https://kefirgames.helpshift.com/hc/en/14-rob-run/faq/1354-how-do-i-make-money/) lists these methods:

- Collect and sell loot
- Haul and flip cargo
- Produce goods at your base
- Join the police
- Settlement owners and clans that control territories also collect taxes

Kefir does not, in that article, rank these methods by profit or publish payout tables, so we do not either. For how heists and selling work, see the [heists guide](/en/guides/heists/). For plots and production, see [bases](/en/guides/bases/). For police pay, see [cops](/en/guides/cops/).

In a [November 13, 2026 FAQ](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/4262460/view/1849192144187218), Kefir said **solo players** get built-in perks, “such as Notoriety level 4 right from the start, along with bonuses when selling goods and collecting loot.” The post does not spell out the size of those bonuses.

The [November 4, 2026 announcement](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/4262460/view/1848557880479055) said Kefir massively increased profits from base businesses, including farms, labs, and contraband; increased cash found in dumpsters; and made dismantling and selling shipments more profitable while reducing drop rates and increasing dismantling time. Those are patch notes, not a current price list.

## Selling loot

[How do I sell my loot?](https://kefirgames.helpshift.com/hc/en/14-rob-run/faq/1349-how-do-i-sell-my-loot/) says to look for a van marked with a dollar sign on your map in Looty Bay, or the **Sales Center**, and sell valuables to the **fence** there. Grab your cash off the counter immediately; otherwise it can be stolen or confiscated.

In the November 13 FAQ, Kefir said camping drop-off points is part of the game for now. You have several drop-off points, and you can scout first. They tried safe drop-off points with lower payouts in early playtests and found the game less intense, so they are leaving the current setup for now.

The same FAQ says it is currently possible to sell a valuable item by accident — for example, they mention that a key can unlock a safe, a lottery ticket can land a large payout, or a jammer is needed to steal the Buletti from the Grand Hotel. They plan to make item purposes clearer. Treat that as a warning, not as a complete item guide; we are not building an item database.

## Banking, laundering, and theft

[How do I open a bank account and why launder money?](https://kefirgames.helpshift.com/hc/en/14-rob-run/faq/1355-how-do-i-open-a-bank-account-and-why-launder-money/) says: visit the **Looty Bay Bank** as a **Civilian** to open an account, then run cash through the **Laundering Desk** at your base. Laundering takes time. The November 4 announcement said Kefir massively increased the amount of cash the laundering desk can hold, but it did not publish the new cap.

[Can my money be stolen?](https://kefirgames.helpshift.com/hc/en/14-rob-run/faq/1356-can-my-money-be-stolen/) says cash can be stolen or confiscated. Banked money is safe from theft until the next wipe. It is not safe from the [Federal Raid](/en/guides/wipe/).

You can only [store cash at Lenny’s](https://kefirgames.helpshift.com/hc/en/14-rob-run/section/132-looting-and-robbing/). Stolen goods can be stored at your base, but other players can raid you.

## Purchases

[B-Coins](https://kefirgames.helpshift.com/hc/en/14-rob-run/faq/1368-do-purchases-affect-gameplay/) buy cosmetic items only and do not affect stats, balance, or progression.
