The Help Center has one short article on clans. Kefir’s Steam posts from before the November 4, 2026 Early Access launch add more detail about clan size and territory. Those pre-launch posts are marked below, because ROB RUN is in Early Access and systems can change. Official pages do not publish a clan fee amount, a member-rank system, district levels, tax percentages, or keybinds, so this page leaves those out.

## Joining or founding a clan

[How do I join or create a clan?](https://kefirgames.helpshift.com/hc/en/14-rob-run/faq/1345-how-do-i-join-or-create-a-clan/) says:

- Open the **Clan** tab in the **side menu**.
- **Founding** a clan requires **laundered funds in your bank account**. The article does not say how much.
- Members fight together for control by taking over **desert districts** and **city businesses**.

That is the full Help Center text. It does not explain how invitations, leaving a clan, or clan leadership work.

Laundered money is covered in the [money guide](/en/guides/money/). In short, [How do I open a bank account and why launder money?](https://kefirgames.helpshift.com/hc/en/14-rob-run/faq/1355-how-do-i-open-a-bank-account-and-why-launder-money/) says to open an account at the Looty Bay Bank as a Civilian, then run your cash through the Laundering Desk at your base.

## Clan versus party

These are two different things in official posts:

- **Party:** a [September 10, 2026 post](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/4262460/view/1843481262692060) (pre-launch) says you can assemble a party of **up to three people**, and points larger friend groups to clans. In the [November 13, 2026 FAQ](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/4262460/view/1849192144187218), Kefir said they tested parties of more than three early in development and are **not planning to expand party size for now**.
- **Clan:** an [October 2, 2026 FAQ](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/4262460/view/1845383656385404) (pre-launch) says **up to 100 people** can join a single clan, and that for now clans **only exist on the player’s chosen server**, which “may change in the future.”

## Switching clans

The same October 2 FAQ says you **can switch clans during a server wipe cycle**. It does not describe a cooldown or penalty. For how wipes work, see the [Federal Raid guide](/en/guides/wipe/).

## What clans fight over

Kefir’s posts describe clans as the way to control territory above the level of a single settlement:

- The Help Center says clans that control territories **collect taxes** ([How do I make money?](https://kefirgames.helpshift.com/hc/en/14-rob-run/faq/1354-how-do-i-make-money/)).
- The October 2 FAQ says clans can control **property and territories**, but you have to fight competing factions to secure and hold them.
- [ROB RUN: Global Map](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/4262460/view/1844751498222037) (September 24, 2026, pre-launch) says clans fight to dominate entire **districts** and their settlements and tax all local residents, and that the clan that claims the **central district** collects taxes from every player on the map.

## The power pyramid: District Owner and Shadow Governor

[ROB RUN’s Power Pyramid](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/4262460/view/1847920302231404) (October 28, 2026, pre-launch) describes five tiers: Hobo, Settlement Resident, Settlement Owner, District Owner, and Shadow Governor. The two clan tiers are:

- **District Owner:** controlling an entire district requires a clan. The controlling clan collects a cut of all taxes paid to settlement owners on its turf and gets a boost to **money-laundering speed**; the higher the district’s level, the bigger the boost.
- **Shadow Governor:** the leader of the clan controlling the central **Highlands** district. There can only be one in the game world. That clan gets the best laundering rates and taxes every other clan on the map.

The post does not give tax rates, boost sizes, or how district control is captured or lost. Settlement ownership itself is covered in the [bases guide](/en/guides/bases/).

## Clans and base defense

[ROB RUN: Base Management](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/4262460/view/1846651706680688) (October 16, 2026, pre-launch) lists defensive modules and raid alerts “for you and your allies,” then adds: “Don’t forget clans, either — allies can hold off raiders better than any defense module.” The same post says you can move your entire base at any time to group up with friends. Raids are covered in the [bases guide](/en/guides/bases/).

## Not officially stated

We could not find official text on: the laundered-funds amount to found a clan, clan ranks or permissions, a clan bank, how districts are captured, district levels, tax percentages, or whether clans persist after a wipe. If Kefir publishes any of these, this page will be updated with a link.
