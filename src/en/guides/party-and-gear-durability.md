Two questions come up often: "why can't I play in a party of five?" and "can I repair my weapons?" Both were answered in Kefir's [November 13, 2026 FAQ post](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/4262460/view/1849192144187218), so this page quotes that post and nothing else. Source check: November 25, 2026 (verified as of November 25, 2026).

## Party size

- A [September 10, 2026 Steam post](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/4262460/view/1843481262692060) (pre-launch) says you can assemble a party of **up to three people**, and points larger friend groups to clans (see the [clans guide](/en/guides/clans/)).
- In the November 13 FAQ ("Why Can't I Play in a Party of Five?"), Kefir said they tested parties of more than three early in development. With a limited number of players in a location, larger parties **reduced the number of random encounters**. Because they want players to run into each other and end up in unexpected situations, they are **not planning to expand the party size for now**.

"For now" is Kefir's wording. No later official post gives a new party size or a date for changing it. If you cannot join a friend because they are on another server, see the [troubleshooting guide](/en/guides/troubleshooting/).

## Gear durability and repair

In the same FAQ ("Can Weapons Be Repaired?"), Kefir said:

- Weapons **cannot be repaired at the moment**.
- **Gear loses durability every time you die.** Kefir describes this as one of the mechanics that acts as an **item sink** to keep the in-game economy balanced.
- A **weapon repair mechanic is already planned** for future updates.

## Not officially stated

We found no official text on how much durability is lost per death, what happens when durability reaches zero, which items count as "gear", or when repair will arrive. We do not guess at any of these. Death and loot loss in general are touched on in the [first-hour guide](/en/guides/beginner/) and the [heists guide](/en/guides/heists/).
