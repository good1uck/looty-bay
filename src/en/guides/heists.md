## How a heist starts

[How do I pull off heists in Looty Bay?](https://kefirgames.helpshift.com/hc/en/14-rob-run/faq/1347-how-do-i-pull-off-heists-in-looty-bay/) says:

Putting on a **mask** lets you commit robberies in Looty Bay, but the Police and other Criminals can attack you, and if you die you will lose everything in your bag. You can only clear your Criminal status by **leaving the city**. The next time you enter, you can choose your role again.

Before masking up, scout the building and your getaway route. Keep the staff at gunpoint during the heist so they don’t raise the alarm. With teammates, the Help Center says it is easier to split tasks: breaking in, hauling loot, and providing cover.

The Help Center does not publish a building-by-building walkthrough (bank, gun shop, and so on), so this site does not invent one. The Steam store page mentions bank robberies in its marketing copy, but that is not a walkthrough.

## What happens if you die or get caught

In Looty Bay, [Criminals lose their inventory on death](https://kefirgames.helpshift.com/hc/en/14-rob-run/faq/1344-what-do-i-lose-when-i-die/), but can surrender to the Police to save equipped gear. [Why surrender to the cops?](https://kefirgames.helpshift.com/hc/en/14-rob-run/section/133-police-and-arrests/) The Help Center says you can surrender: your bag contents are confiscated, but you keep your equipped gear. If you have money in the bank, you can offer a **bribe**; if the cop accepts, you keep everything in your bag, and the cop gets a payout.

## Selling the take

Look for a van marked with a dollar sign on your map in Looty Bay, or the Sales Center, and sell to the fence. [Grab the cash off the counter immediately](https://kefirgames.helpshift.com/hc/en/14-rob-run/faq/1349-how-do-i-sell-my-loot/), or it can be stolen or confiscated. You can only store cash at Lenny’s. See the [money guide](/en/guides/money/) for banking and laundering.

Kefir has said that camping drop-off points is currently part of the game, and that you have several points to choose from.

## Notoriety

The [Looting and Robbing](https://kefirgames.helpshift.com/hc/en/14-rob-run/section/132-looting-and-robbing/) section says that going on a heist streak during a single visit to the city raises your **Notoriety** level. The higher it is, the better deals you get from fences. The Help Center excerpt we have does not list every level or the exact payout multipliers, so we do not invent them.

In the [November 13, 2026 FAQ](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/4262460/view/1849192144187218), Kefir said solo players already get Notoriety level 4 from the start, plus bonuses when selling goods and collecting loot. They also said criminal vehicles are more maneuverable than police cruisers, and that ramps around the city can help you shake a chase — and that you can scout a route, hide a getaway car, disable alarms, or intimidate NPCs rather than take a shootout with several cops.

## Parties

In that same FAQ, Kefir said they tested parties of more than three people and are **not planning to expand party size for now**, because larger parties reduced random encounters. They did not publish a full party-UI guide in that post.
