Short answers. Each one links to the longer guide and to the official page we used.

## Is ROB RUN free?

Yes. The [Steam store page](https://store.steampowered.com/app/4262460/ROB_RUN/) lists it as free to play. Kefir also says it does not plan to change the price when the game leaves Early Access. Optional [B-Coins](https://kefirgames.helpshift.com/hc/en/14-rob-run/faq/1368-do-purchases-affect-gameplay/) buy cosmetic items only and do not affect stats, balance, or progression. See [What is ROB RUN](/en/guides/what-is-rob-run/).

## Is it on PS5, Xbox, or a phone?

The Steam page lists system requirements only for 64-bit Windows 10 or later. We have not found an official console or mobile release on the Steam page, the [official site](https://robrun.com/en), or the Help Center. See [What is ROB RUN](/en/guides/what-is-rob-run/).

## Are there redeem codes?

We have not found an official redeem-code or gift-code program in the Help Center, on the Steam store page, or in Kefir’s published Steam FAQ. Ignore third-party “code generators.” See [Are there official codes?](/en/guides/codes/).

## What is the Federal Raid?

Kefir’s Help Center says that every seven days, plots, bases, weapons, and cash are wiped and converted into jail time and authority. Cosmetics, your player name, tutorial progress, settings, and accumulated jail time carry over. See [Federal Raid / wipe](/en/guides/wipe/).

## Which server regions exist?

In a [November 4, 2026 Steam announcement](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/4262460/view/1848557880479055), Kefir said the game currently has two regions, **EU** and **East US**, and that more regions will be added when they can. In a [November 13, 2026 FAQ](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/4262460/view/1849192144187218), they said expansion to other regions such as Asia or Oceania “won't be possible until at least the end of this year.” See the [wipe and servers guide](/en/guides/wipe/).

## How do I play with a friend on another server?

The Help Center says: open the global map, select the Prison location, and click “Surrender to Feds.” Once inside, switch to your friend’s server and resend the party invite. A [November 20, 2026 patch](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/4262460/view/1849827110432538) also added online friends to the servers list. See [troubleshooting](/en/guides/troubleshooting/).

## Will the game keep getting updates?

Yes. The Help Center says the game will receive regular updates, posted in official communities. The Steam Early Access description says Kefir expects about 6–12 months in Early Access, depending on feedback and development. See [What is ROB RUN](/en/guides/what-is-rob-run/).
