**Looty Bay Manual** is an independent, unofficial guide to [ROB RUN](https://store.steampowered.com/app/4262460/ROB_RUN/), a free-to-play Early Access crime sandbox by Kefir. The Chinese name of this site is **Looty Bay 攻略手册**.

This site is **not affiliated with, endorsed by, or operated by Kefir**. ROB RUN, Looty Bay, and other in-game names are used only to identify the game. Trademarks remain with their owners.

## Editorial rules

- Every factual claim about the game is cited to an official source: the [Steam store page](https://store.steampowered.com/app/4262460/ROB_RUN/), [Steam news](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/4262460), the [Kefir Help Center](https://kefirgames.helpshift.com/hc/en/14-rob-run/), or [robrun.com](https://robrun.com/en).
- We do not invent mechanics, numbers, keybinds, maps, or item databases.
- If an official source does not state something, we say so or leave it out.
- Because the game is in Early Access, each guide is dated. A later patch can change a rule.
- There is no official Chinese client. Chinese pages keep official English terms in parentheses on first use.

## Ads and affiliation

This version of the site has **no advertising and no affiliate links**. We do not sell items, accounts, or power-leveling. Kefir’s [terms of use](https://kefir.games/terms-of-service) prohibit paid power-leveling and item-collection services outside the game.

## Official places to play and get help

- Play the game on [Steam](https://store.steampowered.com/app/4262460/ROB_RUN/) (free).
- Official site: [robrun.com](https://robrun.com/en)
- Help and support: [Kefir Help Center](https://kefirgames.helpshift.com/hc/en/14-rob-run/). Kefir lists support email as robrun.support@kefircyprus.com.
- Official communities listed by Kefir include [Discord](https://discord.gg/robrun), [X](https://x.com/ROBRUNGame), [Facebook](https://www.facebook.com/robrungame), [Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/robrungame), [VK](https://vk.ru/robrungame), and [Telegram](https://t.me/robrungame).

Source code for this site is in the public repository [good1uck/looty-bay](https://github.com/good1uck/looty-bay).
